A simple path from preparation to exam registration

Claude is rapidly becoming one of the leading AI platforms for professional work, software development, agentic applications, and enterprise AI solutions.

Anthropic's Claude Certification Program provides role-based professional credentials designed to validate practical skills working with Claude. Certifications are delivered through the Anthropic Partner Academy and are currently available across Associate, Developer, and Architect roles.

Whether you want to certify quickly or study at your own pace, Tellus helps you prepare efficiently for your selected certification.

Find your fit

Which certification should I choose?

New to professional Claude usage? Start with Associate – Foundations.

Building applications, agents, or automation? Choose Developer – Foundations.

Designing Claude solutions for organizations? Choose Architect – Foundations.

Experienced with production AI architecture? Consider Architect – Professional.

Technical professionals may pursue both Developer and Architect tracks: one emphasizes hands-on implementation, while the other emphasizes solution design.

Your credential

Earn a credential you can keep

After passing an exam, candidates receive an official digital credential through Credly that can be shared on LinkedIn, résumés, professional websites, portfolios, and email signatures.

Anthropic currently states that certifications are valid for 12 months. Candidates who renew on time can complete Anthropic's renewal process rather than retaking the full examination. Your certification belongs to you, not Tellus.
